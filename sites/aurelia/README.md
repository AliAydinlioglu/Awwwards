# Atelier Aurelia (Site N° 01)

> **Neoclassical Digital Atelier & Architectural Commissions**  
> Inspired by the pacing, editorial typography, and scroll feeling of [Pear.no](https://pear.no/).

## Concept & Aesthetic
- **Design DNA**: Neoclassical editorial luxury, Roman architectural proportions, Carrara marble & 24K gold foil motifs.
- **Scrollcraft**: 500vh pinned stage with 60fps intra-frame scrubbed video hero across 4 synchronized narrative acts:
  1. **Act I · The Gaze**: Contemplative stillness, raw stone, statue eye.
  2. **Act II · The Alchemy**: Classical permanence gilded with computational fire.
  3. **Act III · Harmony**: Monumental monolith architecture, Corinthian order.
  4. **Act IV · The Opus**: Value-share studio partnership model & commission console.
- **Interactive Audio**: Procedural harmonic drone synthesizer built with Web Audio API (Oscillators, Biquad Filter, Subtle Pink Noise).
- **Post-Hero Sections**: Studio Manifesto, Monolith Selected Works archive, 4 Pillars of Antiquity accordion, Interactive Commission Console, and Dual Timezone Clocks (Oslo & Athens).

## Key Files
- `index.html`: Semantic markup with 500vh stage container, frosted glass plaques, and accessible sections.
- `styles.css`: Custom design tokens, architectural hairline crosshairs, and responsive layouts.
- `main.js`: 60fps lerped scroll scrub engine, active sticky guardian, chapter rail sync, and audio drone.
- `public/hero_scrub.mp4`: Short-GOP (`-g 2`) intra-frame re-encoded video for instant scrubbing.
- `public/images/`: Architectural monolithic renders and hero poster.
- `previews/`: Visual verification screenshots and mobile previews.
